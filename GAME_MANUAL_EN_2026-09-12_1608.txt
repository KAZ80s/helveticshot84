HELVETICSHOT '84 - GAME MANUAL v0.5.1
Timestamp: 2026-09-12 16:08

GOAL
Destroy the hostile drones, score points and survive as many levels as possible.
Every 5th level is a boss level.

CONTROLS
PC: Left/Right arrows or A/D. SPACE = fire. P/ESC = pause.
Touch: left/right and FIRE. Gamepad: stick/D-pad and A/B/trigger.

NEW DEFAULT CRAFT
v0.5.1 introduces the original HELVETIC DART design. The 11x6 pixel grid and fixed
44x24 px hitbox are unchanged. A skin never changes gameplay values.
Default enemies, bonus craft and boss have also been visually redesigned.

CUSTOM DESIGNS
Use DESIGNER to edit the craft pixel by pixel. STANDARD restores HELVETIC DART.
Designs can be saved, exported and imported. A visually smaller or transparent skin
still uses the same fixed hitbox.

HIGH SCORE
LOCAL HI is stored only in this browser/device. RESET deletes it after confirmation.
A different browser or device has its own local high score.

LEVELS
From level 6 onward, alien rows begin to move slightly asynchronously. The effect
increases at higher levels while patterns remain readable and fair.

POWER-UPS
R = RAPID, S = SHIELD, 3 = SPREAD.

BATTLE
Online battle is not active in v0.5.1 yet. The architecture continues to be prepared.
